Quick Reference — Cheat Sheet
What every section, term, and control means and how to use it
① Interface Sections
SectionWhat it showsHow to use it
Body VisualizerAnimated patient silhouette with organ status dots, ECG strip, organ cards, and summary metricsRun a simulation — organs change colour (green→amber→red) based on ODE output. Click any dot to highlight its card.
Time-Series ChartsFull ODE output plotted over simulated time for every state variableSwitch here after running any simulation. Hover over chart lines for exact values. Peak and final values shown in the card header.
Scenario CompareSide-by-side comparison of two clinical scenariosClick a scenario card to load it into Slot A, then click another for Slot B. The Δ table appears automatically.
Clinical ReportRisk assessment (HIGH/MOD/LOW), clinical findings, and parameter summaryAuto-generated after every simulation. Use as a structured interpretation of the numerical results.
★ Risk Score (DPRS)Digital Patient Risk Score — a novel composite index (0–100)Set parameters, click Run Risk Score. Lower is better. Score below 40 = Low, 40–70 = Moderate, above 70 = High.
★ Dose-ResponseSweeps drug dose 0–20 mg across 10 points, plotting AngII, IR, inhibition %, and DPRS at each doseSet glucose level and renal function, click Run Sweep. Use to find the minimum effective dose.
★ Sensitivity MapHeatmap of Glucose × Drug Dose grid — each cell is a full ODE solveSelect an output variable, click Run Heatmap. Green = low risk, red = high risk. Reveals interaction surfaces.
★ DM IndicesHOMA-IR, HOMA-B, and Insulin Sensitivity Index derived from the diabetes ODESet simulation days, click Compute. Use to assess insulin resistance and β-cell function across glucose levels.
★ Cohort SimN virtual patients with randomly sampled glucose and dose values — full DPRS distributionSet N, ranges, and seed, click Run Cohort. Use to study population-level risk distributions.

③ Key Biomarker Glossary
TermFull nameUnitClinical meaningNormal range (approx.)
AngIIAngiotensin IIng/mLPrimary vasoconstrictor of the RAS cascade. Elevated in COVID-19 due to ACE2 downregulation. Drives hypertension, inflammation, and organ stress.< 0.05 ng/mL (plasma)
ANG-(1-7)Angiotensin 1-7ng/mLCounter-regulatory peptide. Opposes AngII effects — vasodilatory and anti-inflammatory. Higher = more protected.Comparable to AngII
AT1RAngiotensin II type 1 receptorng/mLMediates vasoconstriction, inflammation, fibrosis. Elevated AT1R/AT2R ratio → adverse cardiovascular outcomes.—
AT2RAngiotensin II type 2 receptorng/mLCounter-regulatory receptor — cardioprotective, anti-fibrotic. Low AT2R relative to AT1R = worsened prognosis.—
ACE2Angiotensin-converting enzyme 2relativeSARS-CoV-2 entry receptor. Viral binding depletes ACE2 → AngII accumulates. Value 1 = intact, <0.3 = severely depleted.1.0 (baseline)
IRInflammation Indexa.u.Composite inflammation signal driven by AngII, AT1R, glucose, and ACE2 loss. Higher = more systemic inflammation. >50 = severe.< 5 (healthy)
DiacidActive drug metabolite (Benazeprilat/Cilazaprilat)ng/mLPharmacokinetic output. Determines ACE inhibition percentage. Higher in impaired renal patients due to reduced clearance.0 (no drug)
GBlood glucosemmol/L (ODE) / mg/dL (displayed)Blood sugar level. >120 mg/dL = hyperglycaemic in model context.70–100 mg/dL fasting
IInsulinmU/mLPancreatic hormone driving glucose uptake. Low insulin + high glucose = β-cell failure.5–20 mU/mL fasting
β-cellPancreatic β-cell massa.u.β-cells produce insulin. Depletion indicates T2D progression. Negative depletion % = mass increasing (compensatory hypertrophy).~400 (baseline)
HOMA-IRHomeostatic Model Assessment — Insulin Resistance—Standard clinical index. Formula: (G × I) / 22.5. >2.5 = insulin resistant, >5 = severe resistance.< 1.5 normal
HOMA-BHOMA β-cell function%Estimates β-cell secretory capacity. Formula: (20 × I) / (G − 3.5). Lower = worse β-cell function.~100%
ISIInsulin Sensitivity Index—Matsuda-like proxy. Higher = more sensitive to insulin (better metabolic state).High is better
DPRSDigital Patient Risk Score0–100Novel composite index (this work). Weighted combination of IR, receptor balance, counter-regulatory protection, and ACE2 integrity. <40 = Low, 40–70 = Moderate, >70 = High.< 40
S_SBPSystolic Blood Pressure signala.u.Hypertension model output. Represents SBP trajectory from ANG-(1-7)/AngII transit compartment dynamics.~120 mmHg
④ Input Parameters
ParameterRangeWhat it controlsClinical interpretation
Patient Age18–90 yrsModulates RAS rate constants (c_Renin, c_ACE, c_AT1) via the GLU(t, glu) function in the ODEOlder patients have amplified AngII responses and higher baseline inflammation
Glucose1–25 mmol/LScales RAS production rates. Value 1 = physiological profile, 2 = diabetic profile, other values = direct mmol/L inputHyperglycaemia (>10) amplifies IR and AngII accumulation significantly
Drug Dose (Benazepril)0–20 mgDrives diacid PK. Diacid inhibits ACE via Hill equation: Inhib% = (C^n) / (C^n + C50^n)0 = no treatment. 5 mg is the standard starting dose. Diminishing returns above 10 mg.
Simulation Duration24–336 h (infection); 1–30 days (diabetes)Sets ODE integration time window. Displayed as days/months in the metrics strip.5 days captures acute COVID-19 phase; 14 days shows recovery trajectory
SARS InfectionOn / OffActivates ACE2 decay term in the ODE: d(ACE2)/dt includes viral binding rate when OnOff = baseline RAS physiology without viral perturbation
Renal FunctionNormal / ImpairedLoads different .mat parameter files — impaired uses ke_diacid = 0.034 1/hr vs 0.133 1/hr for normal, causing ~3× higher drug accumulationImpaired renal patients accumulate drug faster — monitor for toxicity at higher doses
GenderMale / FemaleSwitches the patient body silhouette image. Does not currently alter ODE parameters (future extension).Visual representation only in this version
⑤ Patient Status Alert Logic
StatusTrigger conditionsClinical meaning
✓ StableIR < 20, AngII < 3 ng/mLPhysiological parameters within acceptable range. No immediate intervention required.
⚠ WarningIR 20–50 OR AngII 3–10 ng/mLModerate RAS activation. Clinical monitoring recommended. Consider pharmacological intervention if untreated.
🚨 CriticalIR 50–80 OR AngII 10–20 ng/mLSevere systemic inflammation. Immediate clinical attention. Drug therapy should be initiated or escalated.
💀 Fatal RiskIR >80 AND AngII >20 AND ACE2 <0.5 AND no drugCatastrophic RAS dysregulation. Multi-organ failure probable without urgent intervention. Model predicts non-survival trajectory.

Novel Contributions (beyond Barbiero et al.)
#ContributionDescriptionLocation
1DPRS — Digital Patient Risk ScoreA novel composite clinical index (0–100) derived from RAS ODE outputs. Weighted combination of inflammation burden (IR), AT1R/AT2R receptor imbalance, ANG-(1-7)/AngII counter-regulatory protection ratio, and ACE2 integrity fraction. Barbiero et al. never aggregated these into a single patient-level score.★ Risk Score tab · extensions.py: compute_dprs()
2Clinical Phase TimelineAutomatic decomposition of the ODE trajectory into five clinical phases (Baseline → Acute → Inflammatory → Drug onset → Steady state) with key timestamps: AngII peak day, ACE2 nadir, IR peak day, and estimated drug half-life.★ Risk Score tab · extensions.py: compute_phases()
3Dose-Response SweepSystematic sweep of Benazepril dose 0–20 mg across 10 points. Each point runs a full LSODA solve. Returns AngII, IR, inhibition %, DPRS, and ANG17/AngII ratio at every dose — revealing the therapeutic window and point of diminishing returns. Barbiero only ran fixed doses.★ Dose-Response tab · extensions.py: run_dose_response()
4Parameter Sensitivity HeatmapA Glucose × Drug Dose grid (up to 8×7 = 56 full ODE solves) visualised as a colour heatmap. Output variable is selectable (IR, AngII, DPRS, ANG17/AngII ratio). Reveals the therapeutic interaction surface — never computed by Barbiero et al.★ Sensitivity Map tab · extensions.py: run_sensitivity()
5Diabetes Clinical Indices (HOMA-IR / HOMA-B / ISI)Standard clinical markers derived from the Barbiero diabetes ODE across a glucose sweep: HOMA-IR (insulin resistance), HOMA-B (β-cell function index), ISI (Matsuda-like proxy), and β-cell depletion %. Barbiero plotted only raw G and I trajectories.★ DM Indices tab · extensions.py: run_diabetes_indices()
6Virtual Patient Cohort SimulationSamples N patients (up to 50) with random glucose and dose values, runs a full LSODA solve for each, and returns DPRS distribution, risk tier stratification (Low/Moderate/High), scatter plot of DPRS vs glucose, and a full patient register. Population-level risk analysis not present in the original paper.★ Cohort Sim tab · extensions.py: run_cohort()
7Interactive Patient Body VisualizerGender-selectable body silhouette with real-time organ hotspot dots that change colour (Normal/Stressed/Critical/Fatal) based on ODE output. Body image applies CSS state filters. Patient prognosis alert banner with four severity levels including fatal risk detection.Body Visualizer tab · HTML/CSS/JS frontend
8FastAPI Web BackendRESTful API wrapping all five Barbiero ODE models, exposing them as POST endpoints. Enables any browser-based frontend to access numerically exact simulation results without Python installation on the client side.backend/main.py
